Privacy Policy - Nexus - Agent Bridge

Effective Date: 2026-09-16

This Privacy Policy explains how the Nexus - Agent Bridge browser extension ("this Extension") handles your information. We collect the minimum data necessary to provide the Extension's functionality, and we never sell or share it with third parties.

1. Information We Access and Process

The Extension only accesses page content when you explicitly activate it by clicking the toolbar icon on the page you are viewing:

  • Page elements you select. When you pick elements on a page to comment on, the Extension reads the technical attributes of those elements — their DOM path, tag name, visible text, computed styles, and the component name if one can be identified — together with the page's URL and title. This is used solely to describe what you pointed at.
  • Your comments. Any notes you type alongside the selected elements.
  • Extension configuration. The server address you connect to and the access token issued to you, stored in the browser's local extension storage.

The Extension does not read page content in the background, does not track your browsing history, and does not access credentials, form inputs, or any information you have not explicitly selected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Submit what you marked. The elements you selected and your comments are sent to the server you (or your organization) configured, where they become a work item for follow-up.
  • Keep you signed in. Your access token is stored locally so you stay connected without re-authorizing.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

No data is sent to the Extension's developer or to any third party. All submitted content goes exclusively to the server endpoint that you configured during setup. We do not sell, rent, or disclose your data to anyone else.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage. The server address, access token, and current session identifier are stored in the browser's extension storage on your device.
  • Transmitted content. Selected element data and your comments are transmitted over HTTPS to your configured server, where retention is governed by that server's own policies.
  • Authorization. Access requires an explicit authorization step performed while you are signed in to your organization's server; tokens can be revoked at any time from that server.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can stop the Extension from reading any page by simply not activating it.
  • You can revoke authorization at any time from your server's settings page.
  • Uninstalling the Extension or clearing its storage removes all locally stored configuration and tokens.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date on this page and, where appropriate, noted on the extension's store listing.
